## Shared symptom routing
C001–C016 are collected once and reused. Additional shared prompts cover persistent cough, jaundice, oral lesions, breast changes, upper-abdominal symptoms, throat/ear/mouth movement symptoms, vomiting blood, chest pain and urinary symptoms.

## Risk-only reviews
Tobacco/areca exposure, family history, prior cancer, unresolved abnormal investigations, HPV results without symptoms/pending follow-up, chronic liver disease, anatomy uncertainty and urinary blood without a configured kidney/bladder pathway can create separate review items without automatically assigning a symptomatic cancer label.
