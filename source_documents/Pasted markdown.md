# 2. Common questions — ask once only

These answers must be stored centrally and reused by all relevant pathways.

### CR010. Swallowing difficulty

- Unsure

If yes, ask the conditional follow-ups:

1\. Did the difficulty begin suddenly or gradually?

- Suddenly
- Gradually
- Unsure

2\. Is it getting worse?

- No
- Yes
- Unsure

3\. Where do you feel the difficulty?

- Mouth or throat

- Chest
- Cannot identify the location

4\. Can you swallow liquids and your own saliva now?

- Yes
- No
- Unsure

Urgent rule: inability to swallow saliva or an acute inability to swallow requires urgent assessment. Severe breathing difficulty or noisy breathing at rest must trigger emergency guidance, not a routine cancer score.
